ABOUT ME

Passionate about creating digital experiences that make a difference

I'm a professional (-ish) game developer with over 4 years of experience in this field, starting all the way back in high school where I developed a game for the student fair. My passion lies in creating memorable experiences for years to come.

With expertise spanning from game engines like Unity and Godot (tried Unreal as well, shame my laptop isn't powerful enough), to art platforms like Aseprite and Photoshop, and even back-end technologies like Python, I bring value to every project I work in.

When I'm not coding, you'll find me teaching computer science and website development, mentoring upcoming developers and university students, but most importantly, playing games.

I really enjoy competitive programming as well. I find it very... fun, in a way. In this era of vibe-coding and whatnot, it's nice being able to actually pause and think about how I'm going to approach a problem.

FEATURED PROJECTS

A showcase of my recent work and technical achievements

01

GeometriSatu

Unity C# GitHub

GeometriSatu is an educational game ecosystem spearheaded by a lecturer at the University of North Sumatra, Mr. Jos Timanta Tarigan, where users can play educative games to earn rewards that can be used in an arcade-style shooting game.

As a project manager and developer, I coordinated a small development team to meet project goals while also implementing core gameplay mechanics across multiple games in the ecosystem, a notable one being a scoring system that was frequently reused in development, which taught me practical experience in game development, team coordination, and most importantly, the multiple uses of a GitHub repository in a development process.

02

Erudite English E-Learning

React TypeScript AI API Database

This was my first ever commercial project: an e-learning platform complete with a user authentication system, a live database to hold materials and videos, a custom quiz system, and an AI-powered essay checker.

This project taught me a lot about professional website development practices, from the technical side of using AI APIs and database management, to the security measures needed to make a secure website.

03

Procedural Environment Generator

Three.js JavaScript WebGL

Built using Three.JS, this sound wave based procedural environment generator is my first hands-on experience with procedural generation techniques, mathematical 3D modeling, and performance-conscious visual effects, including generating complex shapes from mathematical functions and optimizing particle-like effects for rendering.

04

Unily

Web Development Startup

Unily is a platform designed to connect students of the University of North Sumatra to the local businesses started by our fellow students, which also happened to win our university's startup award that year.

During my time then, I not only managed to build a catalogue website to showcase the local businesses, but also learned a thing or two about how to advertise our, pun unintended, advertisement business to fellow students.

05

Red Baron

Unity C# Multiplayer

Red Baron is a multiplayer shooter game my friend and I built to showcase to new, upcoming students to our university. While my friend focused on the assets, I tackled the challenge of building a working local multiplayer system, which relied on two devices to be connected on the same router, and although the approach was rudimentary at best, I was still able to prove that I can handle multiplayer games.
